Privacy Policy for ManageMe

Last updated: 5 September 2026

ManageMe ("the app") helps you keep track of records such as subscriptions, warranties, insurance, and memberships. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information You Enter

ManageMe stores the records you create — including title, description, provider, record type, start/end dates, and renewal period — locally on your device using an on-device database. This information stays on your device.

No Data Collection or Transmission

We do not collect, transmit, or store your records on any server. The app has no account system and does not send your data to us or to third parties.

No Analytics or Advertising

ManageMe does not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking. It does not collect your location, contacts, or personal identifiers.

Device Security (Biometrics)

If enabled, the app may use your device's biometric or screen-lock authentication to restrict access to the app. This is handled entirely by your device's operating system; the app never accesses, collects, or stores your biometric data.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing individual records within the app or by uninstalling the app, which permanently removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

ManageMe is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact
